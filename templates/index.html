Powered hip and knee provide controlled assistive torque. Ankle operation is passive: pulley + cable + extension spring with optional clutch. Aluminum is the primary structural load path; printed polymers are cuffs/guards/prototypes.

The demonstrated calculations are sizing examples. Do not use the prototype on a person until structure, electrical interruption, regeneration, torque limits, clutch behavior and supported-stance fall prevention are validated.

2-link sagittal kinematics

x = Lt sin(qh) + Ls sin(qh - qk); z = -Lt cos(qh) - Ls cos(qh - qk). Hip flexion and knee flexion positive. Hip origin at (0,0); z positive up.

Planar dynamics and torque worksheet

M(q)q̈ + C(q,q̇) + G(q) = τ. Sample link masses/inertias approximate a human leg for math demonstration only; do not equate modeled net torques with desired exoskeleton motor assistance.

Actuator rating: 9 Nm continuous, 22 Nm peak at rated test conditions. Define assistance torque from inverse dynamics, exoskeleton weight/inertia and desired assist fraction, then calculate RMS and peak along the gait cycle.

Passive ankle spring and moment arm

F = F_initial + k Δx; torque = force × instantaneous cable moment arm × efficiency. Extension spring initial tension may be significant.
